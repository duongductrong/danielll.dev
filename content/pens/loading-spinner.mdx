Three different types of loading animations that you can use in your applications.

<Preview component="loadingSpinner" />

## Spinner Types

### 1. Rotating Circle
Classic spinning border animation with a transparent section that creates the spinning effect.

### 2. Bouncing Dots
Three dots bouncing in sequence with staggered delays for a wave-like effect.

### 3. Pulsing Circle
Circle that scales in and out continuously for a breathing effect.

## Animation Details

Each spinner uses different Framer Motion properties:

**Rotating Circle:**
- `animate={{ rotate: 360 }}`
- `transition={{ duration: 1, repeat: Infinity, ease: "linear" }}`

**Bouncing Dots:**
- `animate={{ y: [-10, 0, -10] }}`
- `transition={{ duration: 0.6, repeat: Infinity, delay: i * 0.2 }}`

**Pulsing Circle:**
- `animate={{ scale: [1, 1.2, 1] }}`
- `transition={{ duration: 1, repeat: Infinity }}`

## Customization

All spinners can be easily customized with different:
- Colors (change `bg-purple-600` and `border-purple-600`)
- Sizes (adjust `w-8 h-8` classes)
- Animation speeds (modify `duration` values)
- Spacing (change `space-x-8` and `space-x-2`)

Choose the spinner that best fits your application's design language!
